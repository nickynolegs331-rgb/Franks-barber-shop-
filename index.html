Years cutting hair on Erie Plaza, right across from the Ramsey train station.

Frank opened the shop in July 2013 with a simple idea: give the neighborhood a real, traditional barbershop — the kind where you walk in, get in the chair, and walk out looking sharp. No gimmicks, no upsells.

That approach has kept people coming back for years, including families who bring their kids in for every single haircut growing up. Whether you're after a classic clean-up or something more current, the goal is the same every time: a quality cut at a fair price, done by someone who's paying attention.
